Title: Intermittent PLC Communication Loss - Line 2
Category: Maintenance Log
Date: 2025-01-08

Line 2's PLC network experienced intermittent communication dropouts, causing sporadic stoppages. Root cause: a damaged shield on an Ethernet/IP cable running near a VFD (variable frequency drive) was allowing electromagnetic interference into the network. Fix: rerouted the cable away from the VFD and replaced with shielded cable rated for industrial EMI environments.
