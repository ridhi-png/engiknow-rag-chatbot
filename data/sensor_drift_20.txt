Title: Temperature Sensor Drift Causing False Alarms
Category: Lessons Learned
Date: 2025-03-14

An RTD temperature sensor on the curing oven began reading 8C higher than actual, triggering unnecessary high-temperature alarms and production stoppages. Root cause: sensor had not been recalibrated in 24 months, exceeding the manufacturer's recommended 12-month interval. Lesson: enforce calibration interval compliance via the CMMS with automatic work order generation.
