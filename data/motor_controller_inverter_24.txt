Title: Inverter Overheating Under Sustained High Torque
Category: Lessons Learned
Date: 2025-01-16

The traction motor inverter on the EV prototype platform overheated during a sustained hill-climb test, derating output power. Root cause: heat sink fins had insufficient airflow due to a duct design that did not account for low-speed operation where fan-forced cooling was inadequate. Lesson: thermal validation testing must include low-speed, high-torque scenarios, not just highway-speed conditions.
