Title: Early Bearing Failure Detection via Vibration Trend
Category: Lessons Learned
Date: 2025-02-21

Predictive maintenance software flagged a rising vibration trend on Fan Motor F-2 two weeks before what would likely have been a failure. Root cause investigation confirmed early-stage outer race bearing wear. Lesson: the 10%-per-week vibration increase threshold used for alerting was validated as an effective early-warning indicator and should be applied fleet-wide to all rotating equipment over 5 HP.
