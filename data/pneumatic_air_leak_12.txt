Title: Air Leak Detected at Quick-Connect Fitting
Category: Maintenance Log
Date: 2025-01-19

An audible air leak was traced to a worn quick-connect fitting on the pneumatic clamping station. The fitting had exceeded its rated 500,000 cycle life. Replaced with a heavy-duty fitting rated for 1,000,000 cycles given the high-frequency clamping application. Added fitting cycle count to the preventive maintenance tracking sheet.
